# Football data source

API-Football is SportPredi's sole football-data provider. The Android app uses its full league catalog, fixture date-range lookup, team/season fixture lookup, league/season fixture lookup, head-to-head fixture lookup, and league/season standings. Stable provider fixture, league, season, and team IDs are retained in Room and connect schedules, results, forms, and standings without name-based ID matching.

The app downloads `GET /leagues` without `current=true`, normalizes and caches the provider's supported league/cup catalog for 24 hours, and offers country and competition selection. The initial selection preserves the prior set of top-five leagues, their domestic cups, and UEFA competitions below; users can choose any catalog entry. Only selected competition IDs are synchronized and shown. The team selector contains teams already known from locally imported fixtures in the selected competitions. A newly selected competition needs an initial sync with all teams enabled before its teams can be selected. A similarly named competition is never substituted for a provider ID:

| Competition set | API-Football IDs |
|---|---|
| England, Spain, Germany, Italy, France top leagues | `39`, `140`, `78`, `135`, `61` |
| Their domestic cups (same country order) | `45`, `143`, `81`, `137`, `66` |
| UEFA Champions League, Europa League, Conference League | `2`, `3`, `848` |

| API endpoint | Local use | Limits and notes |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /leagues` | Discovers provider-supported leagues/cups and their season years; the complete catalog is cached locally for 24 hours. | Users can select any supported competition. Catalog presence does not guarantee that API-Football exposes fixtures for every season or account plan. |
| `GET /fixtures?league=…&season=…` | Initial full current-season and previous-season result/schedule import. | A successfully imported season is marked complete in Room and is not fetched as history again. |
| `GET /fixtures?league=…&season=…&from=…&to=…` | Refreshes the last 14 days of results and next 14 days of fixtures. | Requests are limited to selected competitions; when teams are selected, per-team/season queries are used. Fixture IDs remain the local idempotency key. |
| `GET /fixtures/headtohead?h2h=team1-team2&last=10` | On match-details open, supplements selected-competition head-to-head history when fewer than 10 meetings are stored. Results are persisted with stable fixture/team/competition IDs. | Pair/selection responses are cached for seven days in sync metadata; the call checks `/status`, daily/per-minute reserves, and the shared local request budget. |
| `GET /standings?league=…&season=…` | Supplies season standings and API-Football's recent form for teams in those standings. | Up to 10 per synchronization; normalized rows are cached by API league/season for 24 hours. Empty provider responses are cached too, so cups or unavailable tables are shown explicitly without repeated calls. |
| `GET /odds?fixture=…` | Supplies pre-match odds for one stable API-Football fixture ID. The app supports `Match Winner`/`1X2`, double chance, match goals over/under half-goal lines, both-teams-to-score, and draw-no-bet when returned by the provider. When a complete 1X2 market is available, the displayed forecast blends 60% no-vig bookmaker consensus with 40% local model probability; without one, the forecast remains model-only. Value tips separately combine model probability with no-vig bookmaker consensus when a complete matching market is available; otherwise the offered quote's implied probability is the market reference. A tip needs at least 60% blended win probability and positive expected value. This moderate probability floor filters longshots without imposing a fixed odds band. Integer goal lines settle an exact total as a push. The balance score combines conservative chance (70%) with bounded payout attractiveness, `1 - 1/odds` (30%); the Top 10 adds a soft diminishing-return penalty for repeated market directions, without banning markets. The fixture screen shows up to ten tips for the selected date range (today, tomorrow, the next 7 days, or the rest of the season), using the best price per market/outcome and at most one tip per fixture. | Up to 10 per sync, further limited by the existing batch cap, `/status`, live daily/per-minute headers, and configured reserves. Successful results—including empty/no-supported-market responses—are cached for six hours to avoid repeated calls. |

The performance screen's historical value-tip evaluation only includes fixtures with a saved prediction and saved odds timestamped before kickoff. It reports hit rate, Brier score, estimated EV, and flat-stake ROI for tips selected by the current rules. Coverage is limited to odds snapshots the app previously stored; missing historical odds are excluded, not reconstructed.
| `GET /fixtures?league=…&season=…` | Loads the latest available previous season once, to provide early-season team history. | Incomplete imports retry; successful results stay in the local Room database. |

The selected country/competition/team IDs are stored in Room metadata and applied to both the fixture list and synchronization requests. With no explicit team filter, all teams in the selected competitions are active. Team choices are drawn from teams already imported for those competitions. Each synchronization uses batches of at most 32 quota-counted requests. A `/status` call (excluded from the daily quota) supplies current usage and the plan's daily limit; response headers supply the daily and per-minute limits/remaining counts. The batch prioritizes selected current schedules/results and missing full-season imports; stale standings refreshes use only remaining capacity. The default app budget is 6,500 daily requests with a configurable 1,000-request reserve, plus one per-minute request held in reserve. Every batch remains bounded by the live provider-reported limits. WorkManager runs every 15 minutes with a matching local cooldown; manual sync can bypass only the cooldown. Provider errors, plan restrictions, or missing catalog entries are surfaced rather than hidden.

Fixture history and schedules persist in Room and are updated idempotently by provider fixture ID. Completed full-season imports are not downloaded again; subsequent requests touch only the recent-result/upcoming-fixture window. Team form and the latest five match details are computed from this locally stored history across all supported competitions. Direct meetings are loaded across competitions from local history and supplemented from API-Football when local coverage is incomplete. Standings/form cache rows are derived provider data stored in Room and can be safely rebuilt by synchronization; match details show an explicit not-yet-synchronized or provider-unavailable table state otherwise.

Statistics, lineups, and injury endpoints are not called. Unsupported markets (including first-half, player-prop, and Asian-quarter-line markets) are ignored because the model does not produce matching probabilities or settlement logic. A missing odds response or supported market is shown as unavailable; ordinary match predictions remain visible. A positive estimated EV is a model-based estimate, not a guarantee of match accuracy or betting profit. Endpoint plan access and actual bookmaker/fixture coverage have not been live-verified. There are no bundled OpenFootball or StatsBomb assets and no OpenLigaDB or The Odds API requests.

## Prediction evidence

All upcoming games receive 1X2 probabilities and a most-likely outcome regardless of confidence or historical hit rate. The Dixon-Coles scoreline distribution is shrunk toward the competition scoring baseline with a weight of `effective history / (effective history + 8)`, so sparse team histories have less influence. Backtests split chronologically into training, validation, and a later untouched holdout. Parameter and 1X2-model selection use validation only: the model with the lowest log loss wins, Brier score breaks ties, and fewer than 100 validation predictions falls back to Dixon-Coles v8. The selected model's 1X2 probabilities reweight the scoreline distribution proportionally within each outcome, keeping 1X2, score, and goal-market probabilities coherent. The holdout is never used for selection; it measures the most-probable 1X2 outcome across all test games and reports the change versus the old baseline, even when negative. 90% is an aspirational benchmark, not a prediction filter or guaranteed result.
