# Local Android data and backups

SportPredi stores its working data in the app-private Room database `sportpredi-local.db`. API-Football is the sole source for football competitions, fixtures, team IDs, schedules, and historical results. No bundled dataset, OpenLigaDB, OpenFootball, StatsBomb, or separate odds provider is used.

## Backups

Use **Backup exportieren** and Android's document picker to export a versioned JSON backup. It contains local API-Football competitions, seasons, teams, fixtures, prediction snapshots/evaluations, synchronization metadata, and backtest data. API credentials remain encrypted in app-private preferences and are excluded. Restores are validated and applied transactionally; competition codes from any retired provider are rejected. Keep exported backups in user-selected storage if they should survive uninstall or device loss.

## Synchronization and recovery

- Enter the API-Football key in **Einstellungen → API-Schlüssel**. The credential uses Android Keystore AES-GCM encryption and is not logged or backed up. The older encrypted Odds API preference is left untouched for settings compatibility but is no longer displayed or used.
- `/leagues` (without `current=true`) supplies the provider's full supported league/cup catalog, cached locally for 24 hours. Open **Meine Datenauswahl → Auswählen** to select countries or individual competitions; the initial selection preserves the top-five leagues (39, 140, 78, 135, 61), domestic cups (45, 143, 81, 137, 66), and UEFA competitions (2, 3, 848). Only selected competition IDs are included in the fixture view and regular synchronization. Provider catalog visibility does not imply fixture availability for every season or subscription plan.
- The team selector uses stable team IDs from fixtures already imported in the selected competitions. Leave **Alle Mannschaften** active to import the initial schedule for newly selected competitions; after that, locally known teams can be selected to narrow future fixture synchronization. Competition and team selections are persisted in Room metadata and are independent of the user's text filters.
- With all teams enabled, a season with no successful full import is fetched once using `league` + `season`; successful completion, including a valid empty response, is recorded in Room. This applies to selected current-season results/schedules and the latest available previous season for form history. Subsequent current-season refreshes query the last 14 days plus the next 14 days with `league`, `season`, `from`, and `to`, updating only that moving window. With a team selection, per-team/season requests are rotated within the request budget and completed season markers prevent repeat full imports. Failed/unattempted imports remain eligible for retry.
- Each sync starts with `/status`, which does not count against the daily quota. It reads only `requests.current` and `requests.limit_day`; account identity fields are ignored. The 32-request batch limit applies to quota-counted calls: catalog, up to 13 selected current-season requests, unfinished previous-season imports, then stale standings requests using any remaining capacity. Live daily/per-minute limits and reserves apply to every request. WorkManager runs every 15 minutes with a matching local cooldown.
- The default local daily budget is 6,500 requests with a configurable 1,000-request reserve under the stated 7,500/day plan. The app uses `/status` and response headers (`x-ratelimit-requests-limit`, `x-ratelimit-requests-remaining`, `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining`) to cap requests to the lower of server-reported remaining capacity and configured limits. A configurable one-request per-minute reserve is also held back; every batch is additionally bounded by the live per-minute remaining header. Configuration cannot exceed the stated 7,500/day ceiling including the daily reserve. Manual **Aktualisieren** bypasses the cooldown but not the live provider limits or per-run cap.
- Provider error details and HTTP status codes are surfaced in sync errors after redaction of credential-like values. Neither the key nor `/status` account identity fields are stored or logged.
- Fixture IDs, league IDs/seasons, team IDs, dates/statuses, and scores are normalized into the local prediction dataset. For upcoming matches, the app may also request `GET /odds?fixture={fixtureId}`. When available, it maps unambiguous 1X2, double-chance, full-match goals over/under (half-goal lines), both-teams-to-score, and draw-no-bet quotes. A complete 1X2 market adjusts the displayed forecast to 60% no-vig bookmaker consensus and 40% local model probability; absent a complete market, the forecast stays model-only. Value-tip selection separately blends model probability with a no-vig bookmaker probability when a complete matching market is available; otherwise the offered quote's implied probability is the market proxy. A recommendation needs at least 60% blended win probability and positive expected value, a moderate chance floor that filters longshots without imposing a fixed odds band. Integer total-goal lines settle an exact total as a push, not a win or loss. The balance score gives conservative chance 70% weight and bounded payout attractiveness (`1 - 1/odds`) 30% weight; the Top 10 also softly discounts repeated market directions, without hard market bans. The fixture screen displays up to ten picks for the selected date range (today, tomorrow, the next 7 days, or the rest of the season), taking at most one tip per fixture. Draw-no-bet compares conditional win probability only among non-push outcomes and reports draw probability separately. Unsupported markets are omitted rather than approximated. These remain model estimates, not promises of accuracy or profit.
- The performance screen evaluates saved historical value tips only when both the saved model forecast and odds snapshot precede kickoff. It compares recommendations meeting the active 60% blended-chance floor with a broader diagnostic sample of all positive-EV selections, including tips below that floor; the broader sample does not change active selection. Both show hit rate excluding pushes, Brier score, and one-unit flat-stake ROI. Since odds are only cached for upcoming fixtures, historical results are limited to games for which a pre-match quote snapshot was retained; they are not a full historical market backtest.
- When match details open with fewer than 10 locally stored direct meetings in selected competitions, the app requests `GET /fixtures/headtohead?h2h={homeTeamId}-{awayTeamId}&last=10`. Completed meetings are filtered to the selected pair, selected competition IDs, and dates before the upcoming match, then upserted into the existing fixture/team/competition tables. Results are cached by unordered team pair plus competition selection for seven days; `/status`, live request headers, the local daily counter, and configured safety reserves are checked before the quota-counted request. Locally available duels remain visible if the provider request fails, and the failure is shown in the details.
- Odds responses are cached by API-Football fixture ID for six hours, including successful empty/no-supported-market responses. A synchronization makes at most 10 odds requests, and the shared 32-request batch cap plus live `/status` and per-minute/daily remaining headers and configured reserves take precedence. Stale upcoming fixtures are selected in kickoff order; a later batch can continue where capacity ended. Missing odds or supported-market coverage is explicitly displayed, and ordinary model predictions are not suppressed. Fixture cards and match details show at most one qualifying tip; a tip must meet the 60% blended win-probability floor and positive EV. The UI shows the blended model/market chance and the chance/payout balance; the Top 10 uses a diminishing-return diversity adjustment for repeated directions rather than a fixed market cap.
- Statistics, lineups, and injury endpoints are not called. No authenticated API calls are part of builds or tests; the `/odds` subscription access and real fixture/bookmaker coverage remain unverified until the user runs the app with their account.
- `/standings?league=…&season=…` is used for fixture-detail tables and recent team form. Its normalized rows (including an empty result) are cached in Room by the stable `api-football:<league>:<season>` key for 24 hours to avoid duplicate calls. Match details distinguish a not-yet-cached table from a provider response with no table (common for cup competitions). Migration 9→10 adds only this disposable cache table; it preserves all existing local data and preferences.
- Room migration 10→11 adds only the API-Football odds cache keyed by provider fixture ID. It does not clear local fixtures, predictions, credentials, or preferences.
- Recent team form and the latest five match details are derived from completed fixtures in the Room database across all supported competitions. Results remain cached locally and are not re-downloaded as full history after the season-completion marker is stored.
- Room migration 8→9 clears the old provider's competitions/seasons/teams/fixtures, supplementary-source records, sync metadata, prediction snapshots/evaluations, and backtest records. It does not delete credentials, other app preferences, or exported files. This is the deliberate fresh API-only reset the user requested.
- API errors are shown without exposing the credential. Already stored API-Football records remain available after a failed request; a later eligible sync retries it after the cooldown. The local request cap avoids treating a paid daily quota as unlimited.
- **Datenbasis & Abdeckung** reports only competitions and seasons present in the local database. It does not imply coverage for leagues absent from API-Football or inaccessible on the configured plan.
- Every upcoming fixture receives an unfiltered 1X2 probability forecast and most-likely outcome; no holdout accuracy gate suppresses predictions. The scoreline distribution shrinks toward the competition scoring baseline as effective history decreases (`history / (history + 8)` weight for team-specific evidence). The backtest walks chronologically through training, validation, and a later test/holdout period. Half-life, Dixon-Coles ρ, and Elo K-factor (10/20/30) are selected only on validation by the hit rate of one multi-market pick per match with model-implied fair odds of at least 1.30; log loss and Brier score break ties. If fewer than 100 qualified market picks are available, selection falls back to validation log loss and Brier score. The active 1X2 model is selected separately on validation by log loss, then Brier score. Fewer than 100 validation predictions falls back to the default Dixon-Coles parameters. The selected model's 1X2 outcome probabilities proportionally reweight the scoreline distribution, preserving its conditional scoreline shape within each outcome so derived score and goal-market probabilities stay consistent. Holdout data is report-only and never participates in selection. Holdout 1X2 accuracy is the top-probability outcome's hit rate across all test fixtures, and the report compares every candidate model with the old baseline, including actual percentage-point improvement or regression. The 90% figure is an aspirational overall accuracy benchmark, shown as reached only when measured on a sufficient holdout sample; it is not promised or forced.
- The same holdout also reports confidence-filtered 1X2 results and a one-pick-per-match diagnostic across 1X2, double chance, half-goal totals, and both-teams-to-score markets. The main series uses a model-implied fair-odds floor of 1.30; additional 75%/80% confidence rows compare floors of 1.10, 1.15, and 1.20 solely to quantify how shorter prices affect hit rate. These are theoretical prices, not stored bookmaker odds, and the shorter-price rows are diagnostic rather than recommended picks. These hit rates do not measure expected value or ROI and do not alter active tip selection. Historical betting performance is only measurable when genuine pre-kickoff odds are stored.
- Android may defer background tasks, and force-stopping the app suspends them until the app is opened again. This is device-local automation, not an always-on service.

The API-Football account's current subscription terms and quota dashboard are authoritative for endpoint access and request limits. No authenticated requests are made as part of repository builds or tests.
